# 🎤 Voice-to-Voice Demo Script (1 Minute)

## Setup (Do Before Demo)
- Backend running on port 3001
- Frontend open in browser
- Camera started and positioned
- Product ready (e.g., Red Bull, cereal box, snack)
- Volume at 80%

---

## 🎯 Demo Flow (60 seconds)

### **Example 1: Basic Product Scan (20 seconds)**
**[Hold product to camera]**

**You:** *[Wait for "Scan complete"]*  
**AI:** "Scan complete."

**You:** "What am I holding?"  
**AI:** "You're holding Red Bull Energy Drink, 8.4 FL OZ. It has 110 calories, 27g of sugar, and 80mg of caffeine."

**You:** "Is this healthy?"  
**AI:** "This is high in sugar with 27 grams. It's best consumed in moderation due to the sugar and caffeine content."

---

### **Example 2: Dietary Concerns (20 seconds)**
**[Switch to new product, wait for scan]**

**You:** "Does this have any allergens?"  
**AI:** "This contains wheat, milk, and soy. It may also contain traces of peanuts."

**You:** "What about artificial ingredients?"  
**AI:** "Yes, it contains artificial flavors including Red 40 and Yellow 5, plus preservatives like BHT."

---

### **Example 3: Shopping Assistant (20 seconds)**
**You:** "How many servings are in this?"  
**AI:** "This package contains 8 servings."

**You:** "What's the protein per serving?"  
**AI:** "Each serving has 3 grams of protein."

**You:** "Thanks, stop camera"  
**AI:** *[Camera stops]*

---

## 🌟 Key Strengths to Highlight

1. **Hands-Free Operation**: "Notice I never touched the screen - everything is voice-controlled"
2. **Natural Conversation**: "It remembers context - I can ask follow-up questions naturally"
3. **Accessibility First**: "For visually impaired shoppers, this reads every label detail they can't see"
4. **Smart Detection**: "It automatically detects when I switch products"
5. **Real-Time**: "No delays - instant scan and immediate voice response"

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Emphasize the pause**: After "Scan complete", wait 1 second to show the listening indicator
- **Show multiple products**: Switching products demonstrates smart anti-spam detection
- **Natural questions**: Ask follow-ups to show conversational memory
- **End with impact**: "This gives independence to 253 million visually impaired people worldwide"
